P.S.

Madam, ab iska paper kaand bhi suniye.

Socha tha ek letter likhunga, aap thoda hass dengi, kaam khatam. Lekin aapke liye kuch ban raha ho aur mere saath drama na ho? Itni seedhi kahaani toh honi hi nahi thi 😭

Shuruaat hui ghar mein paper dhoondhne se. Paper mila toh dimaag ne bola, “Plain paper? Chudail ji ke liye? Thoda coffee wala colour dete hain.” Maine bhi apne hi dimaag pe bharosa kar liya. Bas, wahi se barbaadi shuru.

Us coffee colour ke chakkar mein saara paper khatam ho gaya. Main paper ko purana sa look dene chala tha, paper ne meri patience ka antim sanskaar kar diya. Last mein ek hi card type ka piece bacha. Usko dekha aur mann mein bas ek baat aayi: “Bhai, ab tu hi hai. Tu bhi mat jaana.” Aakhir isi pe kar diya 😭

Phir haath se likhne ki baari aayi. Meri handwriting ko achanak itni badi zimmedaari mil gayi ki uski bhi thodi practice karni padi. Toh haan, time laga. Effort laga. Aur beech mein kaafi baar “yaar, ye ho kyun nahi raha” bhi hua.

Lekin banate waqt bas yahi socha, aap jab padhengi toh ek baar muskura toh dengi. Aur shayad ye bhi samajh jaayengi ki main kitna bhi tang kar loon, aap mere liye kitni khaas hain. Uske liye ye poora paper kaand worth it tha.

Ab handwriting pe roast karna hai toh kar lijiye.
Bas pehle ek compliment. Itna toh banta hai, madam.
